## Noticing style evolution

Preferences can drift — someone moves from athletic-casual toward more refined pieces, or a fit preference shifts over a season. This is mostly free once evidence entries have dates: a preference with mostly-old evidence and a newer, different preference gaining confidence *is* a drift signal.

**Surface this only on demand, or when it's directly relevant to what's already being discussed** — never as a proactive, scheduled "here's what I've noticed about you lately" message. That would be exactly the kind of unprompted check-in DESIGN.md's anti-patterns rule out, no differently than a daily notification would be. Fine: mentioning it while already discussing a recommendation ("this fits the more refined direction I've noticed you leaning toward"), or answering directly when asked how someone's style has changed. Not fine: bringing it up unprompted on its own.

Always phrase it as an observation, not a verdict — "I've noticed you've been leaning toward lighter fabrics lately" rather than "your style is now coastal-casual." The former invites correction; the latter asserts something that might be wrong.
